# Popover panel stays where it opens under AeroSpace (#54) Implementation Plan — re-plan 2026-10-18

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** A click on either display's tray icon leaves the panel at the logged `at=` position 2 s later with AeroSpace running; the panel still does not blur and hide on open (#38); its rows and Quit still work.

**What the first build proved (2026-10-18, raw in the build report):** `closable: false` on the
panel makes AeroSpace 0.21.3 classify it as a popup (`Aero.AxUiElementWindowType: popup`,
no workspace), and the panel then stays at `at=` on both displays, through workspace
switches, with the flight above it. Two things broke: (1) `app.quit()` hung — Electron's
`Close()` refuses a non-closable window and cancels the quit; the builder's minimal repro
showed `setClosable(true)` inside `before-quit` fixes it. (2) A click on the Test flight row
hid the panel and fired nothing, and the panel sometimes hid by itself 3 to 8 s after opening
when there was real input. The owner asked for a re-plan that finds (2) first.

**Architecture:** The cause of (2), read from AeroSpace 0.21.3's source and to be confirmed by
Task 1 before any fix: AeroSpace's global left-mouse-up monitor
(`Sources/AppBundle/GlobalObserver.swift`) runs on every mouse-up; when the monitor under the
mouse has an active workspace that is **not** AeroSpace's focused workspace, it calls
`focusWorkspace()` on it, and the session that follows natively focuses that workspace's
window (`layout/refresh.swift`, `focusAfter?.nativeFocus()`). A popup never becomes
AeroSpace's focused window (`focusCache.swift` returns early for
`MacosPopupWindowsContainer`), so opening the panel on display A while AeroSpace's focus sits
on display B leaves the two apart; the first mouse-up inside the panel (or anywhere on A's
desktop) makes AeroSpace focus a window on A, the panel blurs, and `blur` hides it. A tray
click does not trigger the same switch because the menu bar is outside the monitor's
`visibleRect`. The fix keeps the popup classification and quit repair, and before `show()`
does what AeroSpace would have done had the click landed on the desktop: focus the active
workspace of the monitor under the mouse (`aerospace workspace $(aerospace list-workspaces
--monitor mouse --visible)`). After that, a mouse-up inside the panel is on the focused
workspace's monitor and AeroSpace leaves focus alone. This also explains #38's blur after the
release (`move-node-to-monitor` changed AeroSpace's focused window in that session, and the
session pushed native focus elsewhere).

**Tech Stack:** Electron 43 (`BrowserWindow` options, `setClosable`), AeroSpace 0.21.3-Beta
CLI (`list-workspaces --monitor mouse --visible`, `workspace`, `list-windows`,
`debug-windows`), the #38 window probe and the first build's click script in `/tmp/jp54`.

**Spec:** GitHub issue #54 (`gh issue view 54`) plus the first build's gate report (quoted in
the coordinator's re-plan message; its raw numbers are the baseline).

### HISTORY.md section

## Popover panel stays where it opens under AeroSpace — #54 (2026-10-18) — ACCEPTED & CLOSED; commit <hash>

AeroSpace adopted the tray panel as a managed window and moved it to its workspace's monitor
within 2 s of a click on the built-in display's icon. The panel now sets `closable: false`.
With no AX close button, AeroSpace 0.21's window filter classifies the accessory app's panel
as a popup and never adopts or moves it. Electron's `Close()` refuses a non-closable window
and cancels `app.quit()`, so `before-quit` calls `popoverWin.setClosable(true)` first.
No AeroSpace release on the panel (#38 stays intact).

- **Proven:** in the worktree with two displays (built-in 1512x982@0,0, external
  1920x1080@1512,-38) and AeroSpace 0.21.3: each open logged `at=947,38` (built-in) or
  `at=2865,-3` (external), and the window probe found the `layer=101` panel at that point at
  once and 2 s later; `aerospace list-windows --all` listed no panel. Test flight rows fired a
  flight from both displays. The panel stayed open through 11 s of mouse moves and shift
  presses; a click outside closed it. Quit row exited in about 500 ms and SIGTERM in about
  250 ms with the panel open; with the `setClosable` line removed, the app was still running
  3 s after Quit. With AeroSpace quit, the panel opened at `at=`, the row flew, Quit exited.
  Full check `env -u ELECTRON_RUN_AS_NODE TEAMS_WEBHOOK_URL= TEST_FLIGHT=1 npm start`: flight
  on both display rows on launch, a click opened the panel, Quit exited. Fresh review: 0
  Critical, 0 Important.
- **What the plan got wrong:** the plan's cause for the first build's row-click hide (AeroSpace
  switching focus on a mouse-up inside the panel) was not confirmed. A click on the icon of the
  display that is not active is used up by macOS to make that display active, with or without
  AeroSpace, so the panel never opened with focus on the other display; the second click opens
  it. The owner picked the smaller build: no `alignTilingWMFocus`, no `aerospace=` log field.
  The hide is tracked in #56; the overlay `closable: false` question in #55.
