jiraPlane github repo

A paper airplane that flies across your screen when something happens to you in Jira.

what it does

When a ticket lands on your plate, or a teammate mentions you, or someone comments on a ticket you're working on, a paper plane crosses your screen towing a manila tag with the news. Click the tag mid-flight and the ticket opens. Ignore it and it flies away.

It checks Jira once a minute, and it's built to stay quiet: your first day is not a plane storm, a burst of updates collapses into one flight, and your own comments never count as news.

you can touch it

The plane flies above every app, the Dock, and the menu bar, but it never gets in your way: clicks go straight through it to whatever you're working in. The plane itself is the one thing you can catch.

  • Grab it and drag it out of the way. It waits while you hold it, then springs back and finishes the crossing from wherever you drop it.
  • Drop it near the edge it was flying toward and the flight ends early.
  • It tolerates three grabs. After the third it's done with you and bolts for the exit at four times its cruising speed.
where it came from

I already had Jira-Alerts, a script that DMs me on Teams when Jira needs me. Then I saw @conniecodes' flying paper airplane on Instagram and thought: what if I just wire it up and merge the two together. So the plane took over the deliveries and the old script's timer retired.

want one?

It runs on a Mac and needs a Jira Data Center account. I built it for my own desk, but the walkthrough takes you from zero in about 15 minutes, and none of it needs Jira admin rights. The technical story lives in the repo README.
